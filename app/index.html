Can’t foster? You can still help.

Howlback is an app for the Rio Grande Valley where fosters get paid back for what they spend on street dogs, and neighbors chip in to help.

Get first word at launch

How it will work

  1. A neighbor fosters a dog

    They post the dog’s story: where they found them, how they’re doing, and updates along the way.

  2. They add their receipts

    Vet visits, vaccines, flea meds, food, and supplies. Every receipt is checked before it counts.

  3. Neighbors chip in

    Follow the dog, cheer them on, and help cover their costs. The foster gets paid back.

Every dollar tied to a receipt

Fosters upload receipts from vets, clinics, and pet stores. Each one is checked before it shows on the dog’s profile, so you can see exactly what your help covers.

  • Only verified business receipts get paid back
  • Fosters verify their ID before their first payback
  • Weekly photo check-ins show the dog is still in their care

Chip in for a dog you care about

Pick a dog, pick an amount, and it goes toward their verified costs. You’ll get their updates, and a note when they find a home.

See dogs near you

Find foster dogs in your part of the Valley, get an alert when a local dog finds a home, and lend a hand on the help board with rides and supplies.
