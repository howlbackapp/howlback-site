How Chip In works

Last updated October 16, 2026

Chip In by Howlback lets neighbors help pay local fosters back for what they spend caring for street dogs.

For supporters

  1. Pick a dog. Every dog on Howlback is in the care of a local foster in the Rio Grande Valley.
  2. See verified receipts. Fosters upload receipts for vet visits, medication, food, supplies, toys, and grooming. Howlback checks each one before it shows up.
  3. Chip in any amount toward a receipt, from $1 up to what’s still needed.
  4. The foster gets paid back. Your chip-in goes to the foster’s bank account through Stripe.

The Howlback fee

Each chip-in has a Howlback fee of $1 plus 8% of the amount, added on top. You can choose a higher percentage if you’d like to help keep Howlback running. The foster always gets the full amount you chip in toward their receipt.

For fosters

  1. Sign up on the foster page with your email. It’s free.
  2. Add your foster dog with a photo and their story. We review it, usually within a day.
  3. Upload receipts that show the business name, date, and total.
  4. Connect your bank through Stripe, which verifies your identity.
  5. Get paid back as supporters chip in. Payouts usually reach your bank within a few business days.

What we check on every receipt

  • It’s from a real business, like a vet clinic, pet store, or feed store
  • The date and total are clear and match what was entered
  • It’s for dog care, not personal items
  • It hasn’t already been uploaded or paid back somewhere else

Good to know

  • Howlback is a for-profit business. Chip-ins pay a foster back for verified costs and can’t be written off on your taxes.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Howlback never sees or stores your card number.
  • Questions? Email hello@howlback.app.
