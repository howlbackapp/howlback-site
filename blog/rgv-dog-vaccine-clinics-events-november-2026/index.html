Low-cost dog vaccine clinics and pet events in the RGV: Oct. to Nov. 2026

Shots due? Thinking about adopting? Here's what's on the calendar for dogs in the Rio Grande Valley from late October through November 2026. Every date below comes from the group hosting it. Plans change, so check the link or call before you load up the car.

Low-cost vaccine clinics

Saturday, Oct. 24: Palm Valley Animal Society community clinic, Edinburg

  • Line starts at 9 AM. Vaccines and microchips for community dogs and cats.
  • Open to any pet, not just dogs adopted from the shelter.
  • Up to 4 pets per vehicle and 2 per handler. Card payment only.
  • Questions: (956) 686-1141 or pvastx.org/events.

Saturday, Oct. 31: RGV Humane Society monthly clinic, Harlingen

  • 18038 Garrett Rd, Harlingen. 9 to 11:30 AM. If you can't make that window, they can't see you.
  • Pre-registration only, first come, first served.
  • Posted prices: rabies $13, DHPPV $16, Bordetella $16, microchip $11, dewormer $16, heartworm test $20.
  • Dogs must ride inside the vehicle, not in the truck bed. Rabies shots start at 3 months old.
  • Heartworm meds need a printed negative heartworm test from the past 12 months.

Saturdays Oct. 24, Nov. 21, and Dec. 19: PetVet clinic, Mission

  • Inside Tractor Supply, 1501 W Expressway 83, Mission. 1 to 2:30 PM.
  • Posted prices: rabies $36, 5-in-1 (distemper and parvo) $43, plus a $6 disposal fee per pet. Packages start at $79.

That costs more than the shelter clinics, but it runs on a set schedule and doesn't need a pre-registration window.

Coming in November: Harlingen city clinic

The Harlingen Health Department held a free pet vaccine clinic on Oct. 10 for people living inside city limits, and KRGV reported a second one is planned for November. Dates weren't out yet when we checked. The October spots went fast, so watch the city's announcements.

Any week: by appointment

If none of those dates work, the Petco vaccination clinic at 317 E Trenton Rd in Edinburg books appointments online or at (956) 287-0539. The full list of Valley clinics is on our clinics page.

Adoption days

  • Sundays, 12:30 to 5:30 PM: Palm Valley Animal Society brings adoptable dogs to PetSmart and Petco near 10th Street and Trenton Road in McAllen.
  • Saturdays, 11:30 AM to 5 PM: "Adopt Joy" adoptions at PetSmart, per the shelter's events page.

Want a smaller group? Our rescues page lists more than 20 Valley rescues, from Sullivan City to South Padre Island.

Events that help shelter dogs

  • Saturday, Oct. 24, 1:30 PM: Lotería Night, Edinburg. Play for prizes at the Boys & Girls Clubs of Edinburg. Proceeds go to Palm Valley Animal Society's shelter pets.
  • Friday, Nov. 6, 6 to 11 PM: Toast to Tails gala, Brownsville. Brownsville Events Center, 1 Event Center. Día de los Muertos theme, festive attire encouraged. Proceeds go to the Brownsville Animal Regulation and Care Center's shelter, medical care, and adoptions.

Before you go

  • Bring a leash for every dog, and a carrier for cats.
  • Bring your dog's old shot records if you have them.
  • Take cash and a card. Some clinics take only one or the other.
  • Found a stray that needs shots? Our post on what it costs to foster a dog in the RGV breaks down a first vet visit, and who to call lists shelters by city.
