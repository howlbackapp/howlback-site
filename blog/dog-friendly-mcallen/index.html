Dog-friendly McAllen, TX: parks, trails, patios, and vets

McAllen is a good city to have a dog in, if you know where to go. Here are the spots to walk, play, and grab a bite together, plus the pet stores, vets, and city rules every McAllen dog owner should know.

Dog park

  • McAllen Dog Park: 400 Tamarack Ave, McAllen, TX 78501. Separate fenced areas for big and small dogs, so they can run off-leash. Trees, benches, and water fountains for both people and dogs.

First visit? Make sure your dog is vaccinated and comfortable around other dogs. Pick a quiet time, like a weekday morning, so a new or nervous dog can settle in.

Walking trails

McAllen has about 15 miles of paved hike and bike trails, free and open from early morning to late at night. Dogs are allowed. Keep yours leashed and pick up after them.

  • Bicentennial Hike and Bike Trail: tree-lined and paved. Starts downtown and heads north. Note: one crossing at the expressway is at street level, with crossing buttons on each side.
  • 2nd Street Hike and Bike Trail: paved, running through more residential parts of the city. Easy and flat.

Dog-friendly patios

These places are listed as dog-friendly on BringFido. Policies can change, so call ahead, and keep your pup leashed.

  • Love Letter Coffee: 7824 N Ware Rd #1, McAllen, TX 78504. (956) 627-0675. Fully fenced backyard patio for vaccinated pups, and they have pup cups.
  • Schneider's German Gasthaus & Beergarden: 5507 N Ware Rd, McAllen, TX 78504. (956) 242-0335. Leashed dogs welcome in the beer garden only.
  • Brocheta Bistro: 3400 W Expressway 83 #700, McAllen, TX 78501. (956) 540-2979. Shaded, fenced side patio with water bowls.
  • The Patio on Guerra: 116 S 17th St, McAllen, TX 78501. (956) 661-9100. Pet-friendly tables on the outdoor patio.
  • Roosevelt's at 7: 821 N Main St, McAllen, TX 78502. (956) 928-1994. Dog-friendly patio seating.
  • Bodega Tavern & Kitchen: 2901 N 10th St, McAllen, TX 78501. (956) 627-0482. Pet-friendly outdoor tables.

Pet supplies

  • PetSmart (north McAllen): 7600 N 10th St, Bldg 200, McAllen, TX 78504. (956) 686-9037
  • PetSmart (south McAllen): 420 E Expressway 83, McAllen, TX 78503. (956) 618-2203

Vets

  • Pet Doctor 911: 7017 N 10th St, Ste T, McAllen, TX 78504. (956) 683-7387. Listed as open 24 hours. Call ahead in an emergency so they're ready for you.
  • RGV Low Cost Spay/Neuter Clinic: 500 E Hackberry Ave, McAllen, TX 78501. (956) 687-7728. Open Tuesday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Saturday mornings.

Looking for cheap vaccines or a spay/neuter day? See upcoming low-cost clinics in the Valley.

McAllen dog rules to know

  • No running loose. City rules say dogs can't “run at large” in McAllen. A dog off your property and off a leash can be picked up and impounded.
  • Bites and rabies. Any animal suspected of rabies has to be reported to the city. An animal involved in a bite or scratch is confined for at least 10 days.
  • Animal Care Services: 311 N 15th St, McAllen, TX 78501. (956) 681-1919. Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. A standby officer takes emergencies on weekends at the same number. For a true emergency, call 911.

Found a loose dog? Check who to call in the RGV first. Lost yours? Here's what to do in the first hours.

Beat the heat

  • Walk early in the morning or after sundown. Pavement can burn paws even when the air feels fine.
  • Bring water for both of you, every time.
  • Never leave your dog in a parked car.
