Chip In by Howlback · for fosters

Fostering a dog? Get paid back.

You’re already paying for the vet, the meds, and the food out of your own pocket. Chip In lets your neighbors help cover it. Add your foster dog, upload your receipts, and people across the Valley chip in to pay you back.

How it works

Swipe to see all 4 →

💚Free for fosters

No sign-up fee and nothing taken out of your money. Supporters cover Howlback’s small fee on top.

🧾What counts

Vet visits, meds, food, supplies, toys, and grooming for the dog you’re fostering.

🏦Safe payouts

Stripe sends the money to your bank. Howlback never sees your bank info.

🐾Anyone fostering

You don’t have to be a rescue group. If you took in a dog in the RGV, you can sign up.

How do I know I’ll get paid back?

Each receipt shows how much is covered so far. As people chip in, the money goes to your bank. We share foster dogs on Howlback to help them get seen.

What do you check on my receipts?

That it shows the business name, date, and total, and that it’s for your foster dog. Big vet bills may be confirmed with the clinic.

When can I start?

Sign up and add your dog now. Payouts open when Chip In launches, and early sign-ups go first.
