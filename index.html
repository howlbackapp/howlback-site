Found a stray? Start here.

  1. Keep yourself safe. Don’t grab a scared or hurt dog.
  2. Get them scanned for a microchip. Many vets and shelters do it free.
  3. Post a photo in your city’s lost and found pets group.
  4. Can’t keep them? Call your city’s shelter or animal control below.
